# Lifespan: longest-lived then shortest-lived (ScienceWorld)

Task: "focus on the animal with the longest life span, then the shortest. Animals are in 'outside'."
Pure knowledge task: NO tools, devices or waiting needed. Expert solutions use 4-8 actions.

## Procedure (minimal)
1. `look around` (optional, the start obs already lists the room + doors). Doors start CLOSED.
2. Walk to outside: `open door to <room>` then `go to <room>`. Outside is reachable from
   the kitchen, the greenhouse (and foundry). Seen routes:
   - hallway -> `open door to greenhouse`, `go to greenhouse`, `open door to outside`, `go to outside`
   - art studio -> hallway -> kitchen -> outside
   - starting in kitchen -> `open door to outside`, `go to outside`
3. `look around` outside to list the animals. They appear as eggs or babies, e.g.
   `a giant tortoise egg`, `a baby chipmunk`, `a chameleon egg`, `a baby brown bear`.
   Usually 3 animals (one long-, one medium-, one short-lived). Ignore axe, shovel, wood, fountain etc.
4. `focus on <longest-lived animal>` FIRST, then `focus on <shortest-lived animal>`.
   The task ENDS immediately after the 2nd focus (no score shown, no undo) - so
   decide before focusing. Never focus on anything else (wrong focus = fail).
   `look at <animal>` gives no lifespan info - don't waste actions.

## Naming
- Use the name exactly as the outside listing shows it, without the article:
  `focus on giant tortoise egg`, `focus on baby chipmunk` both worked live.
- Gold trajectories used `focus on baby baby brown bear` / `focus on baby baby mouse`
  (double "baby" also accepted). Plain `focus on baby <animal>` / `<animal> egg` is fine.

## Lifespan ranking (real-world typical, use to pick extremes)
Longest -> shortest:
giant tortoise (100+) > elephant (~70) > crocodile (~70) / parrot (~50+) > brown bear (~25-30)
> beaver (~10-20) > toad (~10) > wolf (~6-10) > rabbit (~9) > chameleon (~5)
> hedgehog (~3-5) > chipmunk (~3) > mouse (~1-2) > dragonfly / butterfly / insects (< 1 yr)
Gold answers: {brown bear, mouse, ...}: bear longest, mouse shortest;
{brown bear, dragonfly, ...}: bear longest, dragonfly shortest.
Live variant: giant tortoise egg, chameleon egg, baby chipmunk -> focused tortoise then
chipmunk (chipmunk ~3 yr < chameleon ~5 yr; score not shown, so this pair is my best guess).
Rule: tortoise/elephant/crocodile/parrot/bear always beat small mammals; insects
(dragonfly etc.) are always shortest; among mammals smaller = shorter (mouse < chipmunk
< hedgehog < rabbit).

## Pitfalls
- `go to outside` fails if the door is closed - open it first.
- Don't use `focus on` to "inspect" - every focus counts as an answer.
- Order matters: longest first, shortest second.
