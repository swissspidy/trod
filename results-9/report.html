What we learned

  1. Practice is enough to forge from. Round 8 needed expert trajectories. Here Opus only played train episodes with a success signal (mw reward, mw reset) and wrote what it learned. The notes are rules with actions: “never click a parent menu item; open submenus with hover”, “clicking Submit ends the episode; you must click it, and Enter does not submit”, “the shapes are not in the element list: take a screenshot”.
  2. Notes beat a bigger model again. Without notes, Haiku and Sonnet solve about the same share (68% and 67%). With $0.27 of notes per task type, Haiku solves 90%, at 9% of Sonnet's cost per run and with 33% fewer tokens than Haiku alone.
  3. A forge also finds holes in the grader. No action can draw a curve, so for draw-circle the forge found that one straight chord scores 0.9. Practice optimizes for the reward, not for the skill. Without draw-circle: 70% → 90%.
  4. Rounds 5–9 together: forged knowledge pays in proportion to how much of it recurs: nothing on SWE-bench, little on BIRD, +7.5 points on AppWorld, +37 on ScienceWorld, +22 on MiniWoB’s hard task types.
