A cheaper model with a stronger model's tools

Haiku 5.5 on the round-2 tasks, from scratch (baseline) vs. with the tools Sonnet 5.5 built in round 2 pre-installed in .trod/library. With the library: score 1.000 vs 0.997, at 0.64× the tokens. On code-qa, Haiku from scratch used 491k to 3.7M tokens per session; with Sonnet's callgraph tool every run was perfect at 343–434k, 0.23× on the mean. Tools carry capability from a stronger model to a cheaper one. They did not help where the inherited tool didn't fit the day's question (api-query 1.61×, ticket-triage 1.38×).
